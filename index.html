The SkyRun App

The app isn't in the App Store or Google Play. It's a web app: open it in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen.

  1. Open the app link below in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then View More, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Leave Open as Web App switched on and tap Add at the top right.
  4. Go to your Home Screen and open the new SkyRunZim app. Sign in with your bib number and accept the indemnity.
  5. In the app, tap , then Download map for offline. Wait for "Saved offline ✓".
  6. Allow location and compass when asked.

The Turaco Trail map costs US$20, or is free if you bought it before. You can pay at registration. On course, the app works in airplane mode with GPS on.

Rules that affect your kit

  • 10-minute penalty per missing itemKit is checked at registration, and you can't register without passing. It's mainly checked at the finish.
  • No NSAIDsNo ibuprofen or other anti-inflammatories in your first aid kit.
  • Finish with what you started withLosing kit on course costs 30 minutes. Littering costs 60.
  • Kit changes are announced 24 hours beforeThe race director may change the list because of weather. Weather reports come out a week before and 48 hours before.
